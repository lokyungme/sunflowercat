My name is Kyung-Me and I am an artist and educator based in New York. Over the past five years, I've taught at RISD, PNCA, and NYU, working with students at the high school, undergraduate, and graduate levels.

Alongside this, I've mentored individuals ranging in age from sixteen to seventy, offering support tailored to each person's creative path. This is the first time I'm offering mentorships outside the institution.

Creative Mentorships

I'm currently offering one-on-one creative mentorship sessions online. Each session runs 45 to 60 minutes and can take place weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or just a few times a year — whatever suits you best.

What happens inside a session?

Each session is a space to look closely at your creative work. We begin by talking about what you're thinking, feeling, and making. From there, we begin to identify the recurring patterns, emotional undercurrents + unspoken questions that run through your creative life.

Together, we will explore the symbolic and psychological terrain beneath your work. We might look at an image you've made, a block you're facing, or a choice you keep returning to – and how these reflect your inner life.

Over time you will begin to see how the patterns in your work echo those in your life, revealing what's unresolved or in need of release. My role is to help you notice these signals and to listen to what they're asking for, so that you can move forward with greater clarity.

Sessions are shaped by where you are now and where you want to go. We can build a focused plan around a specific project, or slow down and feel out new directions.

Who are these sessions for?

These sessions are open to a wide range of people — working artists looking for direction, young students looking for guidance with their portfolios or anyone ready to lead a more creative life.

You do not need to identify as an artist to work with me. I also work with people who are seeking a creative outlet but are not sure where to begin. All that is needed is the willingness to use creative tools as a way of looking inward.

This service is also available to all creative types, not just visual artists. I very much enjoy working with poets, writers, filmmakers -- anyone with ideas they would like to expand upon.
