paintings for pkan

In July 2026, my beautiful two-year-old niece, Diana, was diagnosed with an ultra-rare genetic disease called PKAN. Since her arrival, Diana has brought so much joy and light into our family. When we found out she had been diagnosed with an “incurable” terminal disease, it felt as though the sun had gone out.

Heartbroken, I began painting. As one sunflower became another, I began to wonder…

What happens if enough people join together with faith + determination? Can we conjure the cure for an “incurable” disease?

Currently, researchers at Oregon Health & Science University are working on a promising gene therapy. Reaching the next stage will require $3 million in funding.

I am selling these paintings for $100 each. All proceeds go to Loving Loic, a foundation dedicated to finding a cure for PKAN.

Every painting supports the collective effort to find a cure. Every contribution helps carry that hope forward.
