birth of a sunflower, 2025

Studies, experiments, and paintings made on the way toward i, sunflower.

01

in black ink

The sunflower began as a drawing: a structure to observe, repeat, and slowly loosen.

02

color enters

Color changed the flower from a subject into an atmosphere. Yellow, orange, and red began to carry the image.

03

baby buds

Small paintings became a place to try variations quickly—each one a beginning rather than a conclusion.

04

the flower grows

The image became larger and more direct, approaching the scale and physical presence of i, sunflower.
